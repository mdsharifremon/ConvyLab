# 05 What we do: design (v1)

- Light tint background, three equal white cards, 1.5rem gap. Heading left, short intro right.
- Third card (Growth System) is dark primary: it is the fullest offer and gives the row a visual end point. Not labelled "best" to avoid sales pressure.
- Each card: number, name, one-line promise, "Best when" line with a divider, then a tick list. Same order in all three so they compare by eye.
- Hover (desktop mouse only): card lifts 0.5rem, shadow deepens, accent line grows across the top (0.8s). One effect at a time.
- Scroll: cards rise in one after another (1.2s expo.out, 0.15s stagger), once.
- Tablet and phone: one column, max 38rem. Reduced motion: no rise, no hover.
- Different from sections 3 and 4 on purpose: those are steps in a line; this is a side-by-side choice.
