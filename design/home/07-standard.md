# 07 The standard: design (v1)

- Dark section (the form-panel colour) to break the run of light sections and give the page a pause before testimonials.
- Four wide rows: number, big title, short line. No cards, no icons. The type does the work.
- Scroll: each row starts dim (22%) and lights up to full as it reaches the middle of the screen; its hairline draws from the left. Scrubbed, so it follows the scroll. Rows stay lit once read.
- Different from the other sections on purpose: no boxes, no steps, no hover. Just reading.
- Under 56.25rem: two-column rows (number, then title and line). Reduced motion: all rows fully lit, lines fully drawn.
