# 08 Testimonials: design (v1)

- Light tint section. One large quote at a time in the headline font, with a big faint quotation mark behind it.
- Right column: three names. The active one is dark with a thin bar under it that fills over 8 seconds; when full, the next quote comes in (old one fades up, new one rises in). Click a name to jump.
- Runs only while the section is on screen. Pauses while the pointer is over it.
- Different from the other sections: no scroll pinning, no cards. Calm, one idea on screen.
- Under 68.75rem: names move under the quote in a row. Under 56.25rem: roles hidden, names only. Reduced motion: no auto-change and no bar, names still switch the quote.
- Without scripts all three quotes show one after another.
