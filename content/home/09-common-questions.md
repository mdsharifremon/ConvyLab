# 09 Common questions (v1)

Named "Common questions", not "FAQ". Heading: Good to know before you start.
Six questions, written from what we have already decided. No prices, no timelines, no tool names, no promise of customers.

1. What do I need to send you? A short brief: what is not working and what you want instead. A few lines is enough. We take it from there.
2. Can you work with my current website? Yes. We first check what is holding it back. Then we fix it, or rebuild it, whichever helps you more.
3. How much will it cost? Every project is planned around your business, so there is no fixed list price. After one conversation you get a clear plan and price before you commit to anything.
4. How long does a project take? It depends on the project. Some are small and quick, others take longer. You will know the plan before we start, and you see the work as it grows.
5. Will this bring me more customers? We cannot promise customers, and nobody honestly can. We build the site and the follow-up that bring you engaged visitors and real enquiries. When the conversation turns personal, you take over and turn it into a customer.
6. Which platform will you use? The one that fits your business and the next few years. You do not need to know the technical side. We explain our choice in plain words before we build.

To confirm: question 3 promises a plan and price after one conversation, as in section 4.
