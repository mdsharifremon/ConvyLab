# Home, section 3: Our approach

Status: revised after Sharif's clarification on follow-ups; built in website/ (design/home/03-approach.md).

## Job of this section
Answer "what do you actually do for me?" in one scroll. It expands the three verbs in the hero (attention, connection, action) and adds the fourth that makes the site an asset: keep improving.

## Copy
Eyebrow: Our approach
Heading: It starts with your audience.

1. **Understand your audience**
   Before we design anything, we learn who your visitors are, what worries them and what makes them act.
2. **Capture attention, build connection**
   We speak to that audience in their own words, so the right people stop, read and feel understood.
3. **Inspire action**
   One clear next step on every page. Every enquiry gets a quick first reply and follow-ups, until the conversation turns personal and you take over.
4. **Keep improving, keep converting**
   Every month we read the numbers: how visitors behave, how Google sees your site, how many enquiries come in. Then we improve the pages and follow-ups that hold results back.

Closing line: We bring you engaged visitors and real enquiries, and follow up until the conversation turns personal. Then you turn it into a customer.

## Why (so later changes are informed)
- Step 1 (Sharif's direction) is the differentiator: research before design, so the message is relevant. A template site skips it.
- Attention and connection are one step: attention is earned by relevance, which step 1 creates.
- Follow-up process (Sharif): ConvyLab sends the first reply and the early follow-ups. When the conversation turns personal, the client enters. So "converting" is partly ours (visitor to enquiry to conversation); the final customer is the client's. The closing line states exactly that handover.
- Step 4 is "keep improving, keep converting" because the monthly review covers visitor behaviour, Google performance and enquiry counts, and also the follow-ups. It never promises customers.
- Plain words, no tool names, "see how your site performs" instead of "track visitors" (compliance). Unproven services are written as work we do, never as results.
- Overlap with section 4: section 3 is how the site works on a visitor; section 4 is what happens between us and the client during the project.

## Rejected

- "We turn visitors into clients": depends on the client's follow-up.
