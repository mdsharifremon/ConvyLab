# Home, section 3: Our approach

Status: DRAFT, waiting for design direction and approval.

## Job of this section
Answer "what do you actually do for me?" in one scroll. It expands the three verbs in the hero (attention, connection, action) and adds the fourth that makes the site an asset: keep improving.

## Copy
Eyebrow: Our approach
Heading: A website should do four jobs.

1. **Capture attention**
   Visitors decide in seconds. We make those seconds count with a clear message, a fast page and a design that looks like your business.
2. **Build connection**
   People enquire when they feel understood. We write and structure every page around the problem your visitor came to solve.
3. **Inspire action**
   One clear next step on every page, and enquiry forms built to keep spam out.
4. **Keep improving**
   Each month we look at how your site performs and fix what holds it back. A site nobody looks at again slowly stops working.

Closing line: We bring you engaged visitors and real enquiries. Your follow-up turns them into customers.

## Why (so later changes are informed)
- Four jobs, not a feature list: buyers (consultants, practices, clinics, stores) do not care about tools, they care about what the site does for them.
- The closing line draws the honest boundary. ConvyLab controls engaged visitors and real enquiries. The client's follow-up controls customers. A guarantee of customers would be untrue.
- Job 4 is the system idea: a site that is looked after is an asset, one that is not becomes a liability.
- Plain words, no stack or tool names, "see how your site performs" instead of "track visitors" (compliance).
- Unproven services (reports, spam filtering) are written as work we do, never as results.

## Rejected
- "We turn visitors into clients": depends on the client's follow-up.
- A list of services here: that is section 7 (three ways to work with us).

## Open items
- Confirm "each month" is a cadence ConvyLab will actually keep for every client, or change to "regularly".
