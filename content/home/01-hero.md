# Home 01: Hero

**Status:** Locked (16 Nov 2026)

## Copy

| Part     | Text |
| -------- | ---- |
| Eyebrow  | Your online growth partner |
| Headline | We build systems that engage your audience. |
| Text     | Our approach captures **attention**, builds **connection** and inspires **action**, turning real enquiries into loyal **customers**. |
| Button   | Let's talk |

## Why

**Headline.** The old headline was "We Engage Your Audience Online". It was
reached after a lot of trial and error, so the new one keeps its core,
"engage your audience", and adds the one thing that changed in two years:
ConvyLab now builds a system, not only a website. A visitor gives a hero
about ten seconds, so the line says what we do and nothing else.

**Eyebrow.** It names the relationship. "Website" was left out on purpose:
some clients only want a build and some want the monthly system, and
"websites built and managed" is what every freelancer says. "Online" is kept
from the old headline so a visitor knows which kind of growth is meant.

**Text.** "Captures attention, builds connection and inspires action" is the
approach line from the old site, kept as it was. The ending changed from
"turning visitors into loyal customers" to "turning real enquiries into loyal
customers". That brings in the enquiry step the system delivers, in four
words. Follow-up, reporting and learning are not named here; they are
explained in the next section so the hero stays light.

**One button.** "Work" is already in the menu above, so a second button
would only split attention.

**"Let's talk".** Nobody starts a project in their first ten seconds on a
site. This asks for the smallest step. The form it opens is headed "Tell us
what's hurting", which carries the must-solve idea one click later.

## Rejected

| Option | Why it was dropped |
| ------ | ------------------ |
| Your website should earn its keep. | A slogan. Does not say what we do. |
| We turn your online audience into clients. | A promise we cannot keep. Whether a visitor becomes a client depends on the client's follow-up. |
| Eyebrow: Websites built and managed | Wrong for build-only clients, and generic. |
| Eyebrow: Growth system | Repeats "system" from the headline. |
| Button: Tell us what's hurting | Too long. Now the form heading. |
| Button: Start a project / Let's work together | Asks for commitment too early. |
| Button: Free website audit | Dropped from the offer. |
| Second button: See the work | Duplicates the menu. |
| "Our system then catches every real enquiry, follows up and learns what works" | Too much for a hero. Moved to section 3. |

## Update: second action (replaces the earlier "one button only" decision)
- Added a quiet text link, "See our work", beside "Let's talk". It scrolls to Selected work on the same page.
- Why: first-time visitors are rarely ready to talk after ten seconds and want proof first. The nav "Work" link is easy to miss. One filled button plus one plain link keeps a single clear choice (hierarchy removes the decision cost).
- "Let's talk" stays primary because the case studies have no numbers yet. The corner button stays a single action.
