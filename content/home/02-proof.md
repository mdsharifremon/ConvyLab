## Why

**Logos first, numbers second.** A logo is proof a visitor can recognise.
A number is a claim. Proof first makes the claim easier to believe. The logo
rows also react to the first scroll, which rewards the visitor for moving
past the hero.

**"Trusted by".** Without a label, a logo strip can be read as partners or
tools we use. Two words remove the doubt.

**Four counters.** Each answers a different question: how long, how much,
for how many, and how far. "10+ countries" was added on 16 Nov because the
buyers are in the US and elsewhere, and it shows the team already works
across borders.

## Rejected

| Three logo rows | Adds height without adding information. |
| No label on the logos | Ambiguous. |
